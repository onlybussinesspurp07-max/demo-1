Help & Support

M.S. Bidve Engineering College, Latur – Student Feedback Portal

1. How to Fill the Feedback Form

  • Go to the feedback form page.
  • Login using your Student ID / PRN.
  • Select your department, semester, and subjects.
  • Rate each faculty member based on the given criteria.
  • Review your responses and click Submit.
  • A confirmation message will appear after successful submission.

2. Common Issues & Solutions

Issue Possible Reason Solution
Unable to log in Incorrect PRN or password Recheck credentials or contact the admin office.
Feedback not submitting Required fields not filled Ensure all questions are answered before submission.
Page not loading Internet or server issue Refresh the page or check your internet connection.
Submitted wrong feedback Form already submitted Contact admin to request a reset.

4. Privacy & Confidentiality

All student feedback is confidential and used solely for academic quality improvement. Your identity will not be shared with faculty members or any third party.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Can I edit my feedback after submission?
A: No, once submitted, feedback cannot be edited. Please review carefully before submission.

Q2: Is my feedback anonymous?
A: Yes, all feedback remains anonymous and is only visible to authorized administrators.

Q3: Do I need to submit feedback for all teachers?
A: Yes, please fill feedback for each teacher to ensure complete evaluation.
